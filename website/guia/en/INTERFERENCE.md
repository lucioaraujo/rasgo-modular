## O que é
INTERFERENCE is a screen lit by two or three coherent light sources. Where the waves arrive together, the light adds and the fringe is bright; where they arrive opposed, it cancels and the fringe is dark. A point runs across the screen, and each bright fringe gives a pulse at FRNG, each dark one at DARK.

## Como pensar nele
It is Young's experiment of 1801, the proof that light is a wave. With two sources, the fringes are regular, one after another. With the third, the fringes group and fade in slow cycles, the moiré, which comes out at MOIRE. Slow, it is rhythm; with a high SPEED, INT becomes a sound.

## Controles
- SPEED: how many fringes per second.
- RATIO: the path difference of the third source.
- THIRD: the strength of the third source.
- THR: the fringe threshold.
- COHER: the coherence of the light, from laser to lamp.
Inputs: SPD moves the speed in volts per octave, RATIO adds to RATIO, RST returns to the centre of the screen. Outputs: INT is the intensity, FRNG and DARK the fringe pulses, MOIRE the slow envelope.

## Experimente
1. Patch FRNG into a DRUM with THIRD at 0: a regular pulse. Raise THIRD: the pulses group and vanish in cycles.
2. Patch MOIRE into the level of a VCA: the sound breathes with the moiré.
3. Lower COHER: the sharp pattern turns to haze and the rhythm dissolves.
4. Set SPEED to 220 and patch INT into the MIXER: the fringes become timbre.
