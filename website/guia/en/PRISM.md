## O que é
PRISM splits sound into colours, as a prism splits light. There are six bands, from low to high, each delayed by its own index and placed somewhere in the stereo field. A hit becomes a rainbow that runs down in time.

## Como pensar nele
In glass, each colour travels at its own speed. Here DISP is the thickness of the glass and CURVE the direction: positive, the highs arrive later, as with light; negative, the lows arrive later, which glass does not do. FDBK sends each colour back into the prism, and the echoes separate more on each pass.

## Controles
- DISP: the maximum delay between colours, up to 400 ms.
- CURVE: the direction and shape of the dispersion, from −1 to 1.
- SPRD: how widely the colours spread in stereo.
- FDBK: how much of each colour goes back into the prism.
- MIX: the balance between the original and the dispersed sound.
Inputs: IN takes the sound, DISP moves the dispersion by CV. Outputs: OUT is the mix, WET the dispersed sound only.

## Experimente
1. Run a DRUM through PRISM with CURVE at 1, DISP at 200 ms and SPRD at maximum: each hit runs from low to high, from one side to the other.
2. Set CURVE to −1 and compare.
3. Raise FDBK to 0.7, with DISP at 80 ms and MIX at 0.6: coloured echoes.
4. Patch a slow LFO into DISP: the rainbow breathes.
