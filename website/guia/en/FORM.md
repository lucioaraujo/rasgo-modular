## O que é
FORM shapes the whole piece. Four sound layers come in, go through an arc with intro, rise, climax, fall and coda, and come out with crescendo, diminuendo, a silence before the climax and the closing morendo.

## Como pensar nele
A form is only heard when every layer follows it. That is why FORM is a mixer: whatever goes through it follows the arc. The arc's tension also comes out as a signal, so the filter opens at the climax and TEMPO speeds up with it. The level never goes above the input level.

## Controles
- GRAM: the form — arc, waves, growth, a lull between agitations, or kishō, where the turn is a hard cut.
- LEN: the approximate length, from 1 to 20 minutes.
- DEPTH: the contrast between valley and climax, in dB.
- GEST: the chance of the gestures, such as the silence before the climax.
- MODE: impose the arc, follow the energy at ENV, or oppose the arc.
- SEG1 to SEG4: how much each layer follows the form. Layer 1 is the essential one; layer 4, the ornament.
- LOOP: after the coda, a new piece starts.
- SEED: the same seed gives the same form.
Inputs: IN1 to IN4, the layers; CLK, so changes land on the beat; NEXT, the next section; RST, start again; ENV, the energy to follow. Outputs: O1 to O4 and MIX; TENS, the tension; SECT, ROLE, CLMX, BRTH and END, the moments of the form.

## Experimente
1. Connect four voices to IN1 to IN4 and MIX to the MIXER. With LEN at 1, hear the piece rise, reach the climax and end.
2. Connect TENS to a FILTER's cutoff: the filter opens with the form.
3. Connect SECT, TENS and END to TEMPO: the tempo follows the same form.
