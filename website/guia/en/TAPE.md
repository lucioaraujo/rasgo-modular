## O que é
TAPE is a tape machine. The sound goes in, is recorded onto the tape and comes back with its colour: rounded peaks, a darker top, a bump in the low end, a swaying pitch and a hiss underneath.

## Como pensar nele
In the tape music of Pierre Schaeffer, Pierre Henry, Bebe and Louis Barron and Daphne Oram, the tape was the instrument. Here it is the medium the patch passes through. SPEED changes the whole character: slow tape is darker and sways more. WEAR wears the tape: the level suddenly drops and returns, and sometimes the tape crinkles and the pitch trembles.

## Controles
- DRIVE: the saturation.
- SPEED: the tape speed.
- WOW: the slow wobble of the motor.
- FLUT: the fast tremor of the capstan.
- HISS: the hiss.
- WEAR: the wear of the tape.
Inputs: IN is the sound, WEAR and DRV add to the wear and the saturation. Outputs: OUT is the sound coming back from the tape, DROP a pulse at each failure.

## Experimente
1. Patch the MIXER output into IN and OUT into the MASTER: the whole patch goes through the tape.
2. Set SPEED to 0 and WOW high: the slow tape darkens and sways.
3. Raise WEAR slowly: the failures start to appear.
4. Patch DROP into an SH: each failure draws a new note.
