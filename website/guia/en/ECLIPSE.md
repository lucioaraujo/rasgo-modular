## O que é
ECLIPSE is the Moon with its two months. One is the month of phases, from new moon to new moon. The other is the return to the nodes, the two points where the Moon's orbit crosses Earth's plane. There is only an eclipse when the two coincide, and each eclipse gives a gate at SUN or at MOON.

## Como pensar nele
The two months almost fit together, but not quite. That is why eclipses come in seasons, roughly every six months, and the whole sequence only returns after 223 months: the Saros, already known to the Babylonians. It is an astronomical polyrhythm, rare and predictable. The numbers are those of the Asterismus sky. SYN and DRAC change the months and give other skies.

## Controles
- SPEED: how many days per second.
- LIMIT: the eclipse limit.
- GATE: the gate length.
- SYN: the month of phases.
- DRAC: the month of nodes.
Inputs: CLK makes each pulse a day, RST returns to day 0. Outputs: SUN and MOON are the eclipses, NEW each new moon, NODE each pass through a node, ALIGN the arrival of the season, PHASE the phase of the Moon.

## Experimente
1. Patch NEW into a DRUM and SUN into a chord: the pulse of the month, and now and then a rare event.
2. Patch ALIGN into the level of a voice: it grows as the eclipse season approaches.
3. Patch PHASE into the cutoff of a FILTER: a month-long LFO.
4. Move DRAC close to SYN: eclipses become frequent; move it away: they become very rare.
