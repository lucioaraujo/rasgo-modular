## O que é
VIA is the signal's path treated as matter. There are seven gates the sound can pass through: resistor, diode, capacitor, pulse, transistor, backflow and loudspeaker. The open ones are combined by their average. Each leaves the mark of a component on the sound.

## Como pensar nele
In a circuit, the wire is never neutral: the diode cuts, the capacitor holds and releases, the loudspeaker has a body and saturates. VIA puts those behaviours between any two modules, on an audio or a control cable. The first six gates come from Antitotem; FALANTE is a resonant body tuned by CORPO. With no gate open, the path is silent.

## Controles
- RES: the resistor: the signal, attenuated.
- DIODO: rectifies; only the top side of the wave passes, with direction.
- CAP: the capacitor, which holds and releases slowly.
- PULSO: the edge only; the signal becomes a threshold, almost a step.
- TRANS: the asymmetric curve of the transistor, together with the capacitor.
- REFLX: backflow, the difference between the signal and the capacitor's memory: a current that returns altered.
- FALANTE: the body of a loudspeaker, which resonates and saturates.
- MEM: the capacitor's time, from 1 ms to 2 s.
- CORPO: the loudspeaker's resonance, from 40 to 400 Hz.
- NIVEL: the output level.
Inputs: IN takes the signal, MEM moves the capacitor's time. Outputs: OUT is the transformed signal, CAP the state of the capacitor as CV.

## Experimente
1. Run a voice through VIA with only RES open: the sound passes unchanged.
2. Open FALANTE and turn CORPO: the voice takes on the body of a small or a large cabinet.
3. Close everything and open only DIODO and CAP: an eaten-away tone with memory.
4. Patch an LFO into IN and use the CAP output to move a filter: VIA also treats control signals.
