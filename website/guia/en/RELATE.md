## O que é
RELATE takes two voices and returns them transformed by the relationship between them. An envelope follower measures when each one sounds, and a verb decides what that relationship does.

## Como pensar nele
In a group, musicians do not play alone: one answers, another gives way, a third catches on. RELATE puts that listening between two voices of the patch. There are five verbs. Shadow: B sounds where A sounded, some time later. Hocket: one gives way when the other sounds. Contagion: the slow envelope of each one contaminates the other, and the two start breathing together. Friction: where both sound together, the mix gains their product, which heals slowly once the meeting passes. Ring: the mix is the pure product of the two.

## Controles
- VERBO: the relationship, from 0 to 4 (shadow, hocket, contagion, friction, ring).
- INT: the intensity of the relationship.
- TEMPO: the delay of the shadow and the slowness of the other relationships.
Inputs: A and B take the two voices. Outputs: OA and OB are the transformed voices, MIX their sum, ENC the meeting, from 0 to 1.

## Experimente
1. Patch the main voice into A and the counter-voice into B, and OA and OB into two MIXER channels.
2. With VERBO on hocket, hear the voices start taking turns.
3. Switch to shadow and raise TEMPO: the second voice becomes the rhythmic echo of the first.
4. Patch ENC into the MIX of a SPACE: when the two meet, the space opens up.
