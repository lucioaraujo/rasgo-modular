## O que é
SEASON is a very slow clock: a day with sunrise and sunset, a year with four seasons. The Sun's height comes out at SUN, the daylight at LIGHT, and the mode and tempo of each season at SCL and TEMPO.

## Como pensar nele
The Sun's height comes from real astronomy: it depends on the hour, the time of year and the latitude of the place. At the equator, the day lasts 12 h all year. Far from it, summer days are long and winter days short. Above the polar circle, there is the midnight sun and the polar night. It is the Aquorbium's clock, where day, night and season choose the mode and the tempo, and it gives long form to the patch.

## Controles
- DAY: the length of one day.
- YEAR: how many days the year has.
- LAT: the latitude of the place.
- HEMI: the hemisphere; in the south, the seasons are reversed.
Inputs: RST returns to the dawn of the equinox, SPD speeds time up in volts per octave. Outputs: SUN is the Sun's height, LIGHT the daylight gate, DAWN and DUSK sunrise and sunset, YEAR the phase of the year, SCL the season's mode, TEMPO the tempo.

## Experimente
1. Patch SCL into the SCL of a QUANTIZER: the melody changes mode with each season.
2. Patch SUN into the cutoff of a FILTER: the timbre brightens by day and darkens at night.
3. Patch DAWN into a trigger that wakes a voice and DUSK into one that silences it.
4. Set LAT to 70: hear the summer without night and the winter without day.
