## O que é
SPRING is a spring reverb. In an amplifier, a real spring carries the sound from one end to the other, and each trip back and forth arrives with the lows delayed relative to the highs: that is the "boing". The module imitates this with a delay loop and filters that spread the frequencies in time.

## Como pensar nele
It is less a space than an object: a metal tank that sounds on its own. TENS is the length of the spring, DRIP how much "boing" it has, DAMP the brightness, DECAY how long it lasts. The KICK input shakes the tank, like someone bumping the amplifier.

## Controles
- DECAY: how long the tail lasts.
- TENS: the spring tension; it changes the pitch of the "boing".
- DAMP: takes brightness out of the tail.
- DRIP: how much of the spring's characteristic dispersion comes through.
- MIX: the balance between the original sound and the spring.
Inputs: IN takes the sound, KICK shakes the tank, TENS moves the tension by CV. Outputs: OUT is the mix, WET the spring only.

## Experimente
1. Run a voice through SPRING with MIX at 0.4, DRIP high and DAMP low: the surf sound.
2. Patch a slow CLOCK into KICK: the tank crashes by itself.
3. Patch a slow LFO into TENS: the echo detunes and groans.
4. Send WET to its own MIXER channel: the spring becomes an instrument.
